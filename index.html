How to Install App

Add this app to your home screen for quick 1-tap access:

  1. Make sure you opened this link in **Google Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone).
  2. Tap the browser menu icon (3 vertical dots $\vdots$ in Chrome, or the Share box $\uparrow$ in Safari).
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen" from the menu.
  4. Confirm to place the app icon instantly on your home screen!
