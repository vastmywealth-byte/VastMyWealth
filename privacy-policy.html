Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Vast Wealth Advisors Pvt Ltd ("VastMyWealth", "we", "us") operates this loan-matching platform for customers, DSA partners and banking partners. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it is used.

What we collect

When you apply for a loan or register as a partner/banker, we collect information you provide directly, including your name, mobile number, email address, address/pincode, employment and income details, loan requirement, identity and financial documents you upload (such as PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements and photographs), and bank account details for payouts (partners/bankers only).

How we use it

We use your information to match you with suitable lending partners, share your application and documents with partner banks/NBFCs and their representatives for loan processing, verify your identity, communicate with you about your application by call, SMS, WhatsApp or email, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Your details and documents are shared only with the partner banks/NBFCs and DSA partners actively processing your application, and with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as our cloud storage and communication providers). We do not share your information with unrelated third parties for marketing.

Payments

Where a payment feature is offered to partners/bankers (such as a paid subscription), payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI or bank login details — these are handled directly by Razorpay under its own security standards.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to process your application and to meet legal, accounting or regulatory requirements, after which it may be archived or deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us to review, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by reaching out through the contact details below.
